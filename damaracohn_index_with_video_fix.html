School Zones on This Coast

This stretch of coastline sits in two different school districts — the line matters more than the ZIP code.

Palm Beach County Public Schools

Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Highland Beach, Gulf Stream, Boynton Beach and Ocean Ridge fall under Palm Beach County Public Schools.

View official boundary map →

Broward County Public Schools

Lighthouse Point, Deerfield Beach and Pompano Beach fall under Broward County Public Schools.

View official boundary map →

School assignment runs by exact address and boundaries can shift year to year, so a specific street can zone differently than the town it sits in. Rather than guess or send you to a third-party ratings site, text Damara the address you're considering and she'll tell you straight — zoned school, district, so you can look into it yourself.

Buying on This Coast

Delray Beach and Boca Raton draw a mix of relocating families, snowbirds, and full-time buyers moving down from the Northeast — which means real competition for well-priced homes in walkable or waterfront pockets. Damara's advantage is local pace: knowing which listings are about to hit before they're widely marketed, and which asking prices are negotiable versus firm.

Selling on This Coast

Pricing a home here isn't formulaic — a Comparative Market Analysis needs to account for lot orientation, canal versus ocean access, HOA restrictions, and how recently comparable homes actually closed versus what's still sitting on the market. Damara builds every CMA from real, current activity, not an automated estimate.

Commercial Real Estate

Retail, office, and multifamily opportunities along this corridor move differently than residential — cap rates, lease terms, and zoning matter more than curb appeal. As a Real Estate Capital Advisor, Damara works both the property search and the financing conversation for commercial buyers and investors.

Land & New Construction

Buildable land from Lake Worth to Lantana and new-construction inventory across the corridor both come with their own diligence — setbacks, flood zone designation, and builder reputation matter as much as the price per square foot. Worth a direct conversation before committing to either.

36 Years On This Coast, 25+ Selling It

Two rebuilds. Still standing.

Damara Cohn grew up in Queens, New York, and moved to South Florida in 1990 — the Queens directness never left, and it's part of why New York and New Jersey clients relocating to the coast trust her read on a neighborhood. She earned her degree in Advertising and Public Relations at UCF, then built her career through two of the hardest stretches South Florida real estate has seen, including the 2008 crash — and rebuilt after each one.

"I call this my Third Act. Not starting over — becoming who I was always going to be."

Today she's the founder and Broker-Owner of Mangrove Realty — 18+ years and counting — and a Real Estate Capital Advisor, working the Delray Beach to Boca Raton corridor — and up into Lighthouse Point and Deerfield Beach — she's called home for 36 years. Earlier in her career, she served as the Realtor for the City of Delray Beach and the Delray Beach Community Redevelopment Agency.
