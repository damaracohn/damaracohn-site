Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

This page explains what information damaracohn.com collects when you visit or contact Damara Cohn, and how it's used. This site is operated by Damara Cohn, Broker-Owner of Mangrove Realty, Inc., serving Delray Beach, Boca Raton, and the surrounding coast.

What This Site Collects

damaracohn.com does not run advertising trackers, ad pixels, or third-party analytics. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which — like any web host — automatically logs standard technical information for security and performance (things like IP address, browser type, and pages requested). This is routine server activity, not used to build an advertising profile of you.

When you voluntarily submit information through a form on this site — for example, requesting the Coastal Insider Guide, asking a financing question, or subscribing to market updates — the site collects only what you enter: typically your name, phone number, and/or email address.

How Your Information Is Used

  • To respond to you directly — answering a question, sending the guide you requested, or following up on a financing or real estate inquiry.
  • To send market updates or listing information, only if you specifically opted in (for example, through the newsletter signup).
  • Information submitted through this site is not sold to third parties.

Text and Phone Contact

If you call or text the number listed on this site, that conversation is handled the same as any direct call or text to Damara Cohn or Mangrove Realty — it is not automated, and your number is not added to a marketing list without your consent.

Third-Party Links

This site links out to Mangrove Realty's property search tools, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms. Once you leave damaracohn.com, that platform's own privacy policy applies — this policy only covers damaracohn.com itself.

Your Choices

You can ask to be removed from any mailing list at any time by replying to any email you receive or by contacting Damara directly using the information below. You're never required to submit a form to browse this site or call/text directly.
