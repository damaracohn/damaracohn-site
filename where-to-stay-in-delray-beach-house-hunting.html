Where to Stay in Delray Beach While House Hunting

Where you stay tells you more about where you should buy than any listing will.

Where you stay while house hunting in Delray Beach tells me more about where you should buy than anything you'll tell me on a phone call. It's the fastest, cheapest due diligence a buyer can do before making a six or seven figure decision.

Don't book the first nice-looking rental

Split your trip across two or three different neighborhoods -- downtown, a quieter in-town pocket, and maybe something west -- rather than staying in one spot the whole visit. The difference in daily rhythm between them is bigger than photos ever show.

Walk it at the wrong times

Walk Atlantic Avenue at 7am and at 10pm. Drive your potential commute at actual rush hour, not on a lazy Sunday. The neighborhood you fall for on a perfect afternoon needs to hold up on an ordinary Tuesday too.
