DELRAY BEACH · GETTING AROUND

Does Brightline Stop in Delray Beach?

No. The closest stations are Boca Raton and West Palm Beach. Here's what that actually means if you're relocating here.

No, Brightline does not have a Delray Beach station. The closest stops are Boca Raton and West Palm Beach, both roughly a 20 to 25 minute drive from here.

Which Brightline stations are closest to Delray Beach?

Boca Raton's station is the shorter drive for most of town, and West Palm Beach gives you a bigger station with more departures. Neither is walkable or bikeable from Delray Beach itself -- plan on driving and parking at the station, or using a rideshare.

Does this matter for day-to-day life here?

For most buyers it's a convenience, not a dealbreaker. If you fly in and out of Fort Lauderdale or Miami regularly, or travel up to Orlando often, having Brightline 20 minutes away is a real asset. It just isn't a train-to-the-door setup the way it is in a handful of station towns.
